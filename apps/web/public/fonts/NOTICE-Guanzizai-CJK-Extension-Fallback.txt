Guanzizai CJK Extension Fallback — modification notice

On 2026-07-15, the Guanzizai project created a WOFF2 subset from BabelStone Han
v16.0.3, renamed the font to "Guanzizai CJK Extension Fallback", retained only
the CJK supplementary-plane glyphs required by the published reading corpus,
and removed font hinting.

The modified font is distributed with ARPHICPL.txt and remains freely available
under the ARPHIC PUBLIC LICENSE.

Upstream font: https://www.babelstone.co.uk/Fonts/Han.html
